# Script 4 — Customer QR ordering and table booking

**You are:** a customer sitting at a table, ordering from your own phone.
**You need:** a table QR code (printed or on screen) and the booking link from the tech team. Some steps need a teammate playing the waiter (script 3).
**Time:** about 1.5 hours.

Do each step in order. **Check** says what should happen. If it doesn't, screenshot and tell your AI the step number. If an option isn't on your screen, tell your AI "G-xx not there".

**Pay only the way the tech team tells you.** Never pay with your own real money unless they say so.

## Order at the table

| Step | Do this | Check |
|---|---|---|
| G-01 | Scan the table QR with your phone camera. | The menu opens with the restaurant's name and your table. |
| G-02 | Browse every category. Use search if there is one. | Items, prices and photos load. Sold-out items can't be ordered. |
| G-03 | Open an item with sizes and add-ons. Try to add it without picking a required add-on. | It stops you with a clear message. With all choices made, the price is right. |
| G-04 | In the cart: change a quantity, remove an item, add a note. | The total updates correctly. |
| G-05 | Give your name and phone if asked. Enter the code you receive. | The code arrives within about a minute. A wrong code is refused clearly. |
| G-06 | If asked to pair with your waiter, enter the code your waiter teammate shows you (W-06). | It connects to the right table. |
| G-07 | Place the order. | You see a confirmation and the order status. Your waiter teammate sees it. |
| G-08 | Order more items a bit later. | It's added to the same table, not a new one. |
| G-09 | Call the waiter, ask for water or ask for the bill, if there are buttons. | Your waiter teammate sees the request quickly (W-11). |
| G-10 | View the bill and pay (test payment only). | The amount matches what you ordered, including tax. After paying you see a clear "paid" message. |
| G-11 | Leave a review, if asked. | It's accepted. You see a thank-you. |

## Real-life problems

| Step | Do this | Check |
|---|---|---|
| G-12 | Refresh the page. Close the browser and scan the QR again. | Your order and cart are still there, or you get a sensible message. |
| G-13 | A second person scans the same table QR on their phone and orders too. | Both of you see the same table order. Nothing is doubled. |
| G-14 | Tap **Place order** twice quickly. | Only one order is placed. |
| G-15 | Switch to mobile data or a weak signal while ordering. | A clear message if it fails. No double orders once it works again. |
| G-16 | Scan a **different** table's QR after ordering. | You don't see the first table's order on the second table. |

## Table booking

| Step | Do this | Check |
|---|---|---|
| G-17 | Open the booking link. Book a table for tomorrow, 8 pm, 2 people. | You see a confirmation. Any SMS or email arrives. |
| G-18 | Try to book: a past date, 0 people, 100 people, or no phone number. | Each is stopped with a clear message. |
| G-19 | Ask your owner teammate to check the booking appears in Reservations. | It's there with the right date, time and people. |

**Done?** Tell your AI: "Finished customer script." Mention any steps you skipped.
