# Script 5 — Together (two people)

These checks need two testers at the same time, each on their own phone or laptop and their own login. Never have both people sign in with the same login.

Agree who does what before you start. **Both** tell your own AI the step number if something goes wrong.

| Step | Who | Do this | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-01 | Owner + customer | The customer opens the menu and adds `TEST Lemon Soda` to the cart, without ordering. The owner marks it sold out. The customer tries to order. | The customer is told it's sold out, and is not charged for it. |
| T-02 | Owner + customer | The customer has an item in the cart. The owner changes its price. The customer orders. | The customer sees the new price clearly before paying, or a message about the change. |
| T-03 | Waiter + customer | The waiter opens a table and shows the pairing code. The customer pairs and orders. | The waiter sees the customer's order on the right table. |
| T-04 | Waiter + customer | The customer calls the waiter. | The waiter gets it within a few seconds, on the right table. |
| T-05 | Two waiters | Both open the **same** table. Each adds a different item at the same time. | Both items are on the table once each. Nothing is lost or doubled. |
| T-06 | Cashier + waiter | The waiter's table asks for the bill. The cashier and the waiter both try to settle it at the same moment. | It's paid only once. The other person gets a clear message. |
| T-07 | Owner + new staff | The owner invites a new staff member. The new person accepts on their own phone and signs in. | They get in and only see what their role allows. |
| T-08 | Owner + staff | While the staff member is signed in, the owner removes their access. The staff member tries to carry on. | They are stopped, or signed out, at their next action. |
| T-09 | Two customers | Two phones scan the same table QR. Both add items and order. | One shared table order. Both phones show the same items. |
| T-10 | Owner + cashier | At the end of the day, compare the owner's Analytics for today with the cashier's sales. | The totals match. |

**Done?** Each person tells their own AI: "Finished together script, steps … passed."
