# Script 3 — Waiter app

**You are:** a waiter on the floor, using the waiter app on an Android phone.
**You need:** the waiter app (install link or web link from the tech team) and the waiter login.
**Time:** about 2 hours. Some steps need a teammate playing the customer (script 4).

Do each step in order. **Check** says what should happen. If it doesn't, screenshot and tell your AI the step number. If an option isn't on your screen, tell your AI "W-xx not there".

## Start of shift

| Step | Do this | Check |
|---|---|---|
| W-01 | Open the waiter app. Sign in with your waiter login. | A code arrives within about a minute if one is needed. You get in. |
| W-02 | Choose your outlet, if asked. | The right restaurant and outlet name is shown. |
| W-03 | Clock in or start your shift. | It shows you're on shift, with the right time. |

## Tables and orders

| Step | Do this | Check |
|---|---|---|
| W-04 | Open the floor. Look at all the tables. | Tables and their colours or status match what's really happening. |
| W-05 | Open a free table. Enter the number of guests. | The table turns busy. |
| W-06 | Show the table's pairing code or QR to your "customer" teammate. | The code is clear and readable. Your teammate can use it (script 4, G-06). |
| W-07 | Take an order: 3 items, one with size and add-on, plus a note like "less spicy". Send it to the kitchen. | You get a confirmation. Prices are right. The note is kept. |
| W-08 | Open the kitchen tab. Find the order. Mark it ready. | The order is there with the note. Its status changes. |
| W-09 | Add a second round to the same table. | It goes to the same table, not a new one. |
| W-10 | Cancel one item that was already sent to the kitchen. | It asks for a reason or permission. The total updates. |
| W-11 | When your teammate calls the waiter from their phone, open Requests. | The request appears quickly, on the right table. You can mark it handled. |
| W-12 | Open the table's bill. | The items, tax and total are correct. |
| W-13 | Take a takeaway order from the app, if there's an option. | It's created and shows in Orders. |
| W-14 | Open the Orders tab. Use any filters. | Your orders are listed with the right status. |

## Mistakes and real life

| Step | Do this | Check |
|---|---|---|
| W-15 | Tap **Send** twice quickly. | The order goes only once. |
| W-16 | Lock the phone for 5 minutes, then unlock and carry on. | The app still works. Nothing is lost or doubled. |
| W-17 | Switch from Wi-Fi to mobile data, or walk to a weak-signal spot, while sending an order. | A clear message if it can't send. No double orders after it reconnects. |
| W-18 | Turn the phone sideways. Use the app on the smallest phone you have. | Everything is readable and buttons are easy to tap. |

## Your own things

| Step | Do this | Check |
|---|---|---|
| W-19 | Open your roster, apply for leave, look at your performance, notifications, profile and settings. | Each screen opens and shows your own information. |
| W-20 | Clock out or end your shift. | It records the right time. |
| W-21 | Sign out. | You cannot see tables or orders until you sign in again. |

**Done?** Tell your AI: "Finished waiter script." Mention any steps you skipped.
